# Credits and upstream sources

Build on visible lineage and give upstream work its due. Clear attribution makes the operating practice easier to inspect, maintain, and carry forward. This public surface brings together the delivery and fleet work with the projects and tools that make it possible.

## Shipped and adapted

- **Oracle** — the [Oracle skill](/skills/oracle/) adapts the lifecycle pattern from [Peter Steinberger's oracle](https://github.com/steipete/oracle), MIT.
- **Thermos review family** — [Thermos](/skills/thermos/) supplies optional review lenses tracked from [Cursor plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins), MIT.

## Workflow and harness tools

- [Compound Engineering (3.20.0+)](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin) supplies selected delivery workflow stages and owns review settlement and CI. Resolve it when a selected stage needs it.
- [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com) and [Codex](https://openai.com/codex) supply the harness and plugin surfaces.
- [gh-stack](https://github.com/github/gh-stack) supports dependent-PR delivery.

## Other attribution

- [Ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail) is credited for efficiency guidance; it is not a Railyard prerequisite.

## Fleet foundations

- [Jujutsu](https://jj-vcs.github.io/jj/) supplies the colocated store, conflict state, operation log, and change identity.
- [OpenSSH](https://www.openssh.com/) supplies signing, certificates, key verification, and transport primitives.
- [Homebrew](https://brew.sh/), [APT](https://wiki.debian.org/Apt), and [winget](https://github.com/microsoft/winget-cli) supply native package management.
- [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) supplies the optional transport integration.
- [ShellCheck](https://www.shellcheck.net/) supports shell quality checks in source repositories.

## Licensing

Carry licensing through the same evidence discipline. License obligations and third-party notices remain with the source repositories. This page gives reader-facing attribution and links to the upstream projects.

Corrections are welcome through the [Railyard source repository](https://github.com/novotnyllc/railyard).
