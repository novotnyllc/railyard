# Delivery

Delivery turns a request into a bounded, reviewed, merged, and observable result. Every stage has a useful handoff: a route, a change, a review packet, a settled merge, or proof that the result reached its consumer.

- [Lifecycle](/delivery/lifecycle/) — one request from intent to proof.
- [Model routing](/delivery/model-routing/) — model, effort, carrier, budget, privacy, and transport.
- [Gates](/delivery/gates/) — review settlement, merge authority, and post-merge proof.
- [Audit](/delivery/audit/) — the run record and retrospective that explain how the result arrived.

The [ship a change scenario](/what-it-does/ship-a-change/) is the shortest introduction.

Project evidence lives in the [Railyard repository](https://github.com/novotnyllc/railyard), with [releases](https://github.com/novotnyllc/railyard/releases) and the [review trail](https://github.com/novotnyllc/railyard/pulls) available beside the reader-facing guide.
